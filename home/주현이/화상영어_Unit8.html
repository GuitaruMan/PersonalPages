Day 1꼭 알아야 할 단어 ①

1
subscribe[섭스크라이브]동사
구독하다
I know you subscribe to the magazine monthly.
2
monthly[먼슬리]부사
한 달에 한 번, 매달
She subscribes to the magazine monthly.
3
issue[이슈]명사
(잡지의) 호
Did this month's issue come out already?
4
feature[피처]명사
특집 (기사)
There are a lot of interesting feature articles.
5
relieve[릴리브]동사
(스트레스를) 풀어 주다
It's the best way to relieve my stress.
6
stress[스트레스]명사
스트레스
Reading magazines relieves Sophia's stress.
7
mass media[매스 미디어]명사
대중 매체
The mass media makes it possible for us to learn about cultures.
8
overlook[오버룩]동사
못 본 척 넘기다
But we can't overlook its negative effects.
9
negative[네거티브]형용사
부정적인, 나쁜
We can't overlook its negative effects.
10
effect[이펙트]명사
영향, 효과
The mass media has negative effects, too.
11
exactly[이그잭틀리]부사
바로, 정확히
That's exactly what I'm trying to say.
12
trend[트렌드]명사
유행
People tend to follow trends.

Day 2꼭 알아야 할 단어 ②

13
personality[퍼스낼리티]명사
개성, 성격
It's hard to see people showing their own personalities.
14
exaggerated[이그재저레이티드]형용사
과장된
We can see exaggerated advertisements.
15
advertisement[애드버타이즈먼트]명사
광고
Exaggerated advertisements are on the mass media.
16
influence[인플루언스]동사
영향을 주다
They tend to influence on our decision-making.
17
product[프로덕트]명사
제품, 상품
We see ads when we buy products.
18
consumer[컨슈머]명사
소비자
We should try to become wise consumers.
19
wise[와이즈]형용사
현명한
We should try to become wise consumers.
20
article[아티클]명사
기사, 글
I should write a special article about it.
21
deserve[디저브]동사
~할 자격이 있다
Students deserve to know what is right or wrong.
22
appreciate[어프리시에이트]동사
고맙게 여기다
I appreciate your opinion.
23
opinion[오피니언]명사
의견, 생각
I appreciate your opinion. It will be a big help.
24
advantage[어드밴티지]명사
장점 (↔ disadvantage 단점)
What are some advantages of the mass media?

Day 3꼭 외워야 할 숙어

25
subscribe to ~[섭스크라이브 투]
~을 구독하다
I know you subscribe to the magazine monthly.
26
come out[컴 아웃]
(잡지·책이) 나오다
Did this month's issue come out already?
27
come up with ~[컴 업 위드]
(생각을) 떠올리다
It helps me come up with new ideas.
28
make it possible for A to ~[메이크 잇 파서블 포]
A가 ~할 수 있게 해 주다
The mass media makes it possible for us to learn about cultures.
29
tend to ~[텐드 투]
~하는 경향이 있다
People tend to follow trends.
30
point of view[포인트 오브 뷰]
관점, 보는 시각
Everyone seems to have the same points of view.
31
try to ~[트라이 투]
~하려고 애쓰다
We should try to become wise consumers.
32
That's exactly what I'm trying to say.[댓츠 이그잭틀리 왓 아임 트라잉 투 세이]
그게 바로 내가 하려던 말이야.
You're right. That's exactly what I'm trying to say.
33
That's a good point.[댓츠 어 굿 포인트]
좋은 지적이야.
That's a good point. I'm with you on that.
34
speaking of ~[스피킹 오브]
~ 얘기가 나와서 말인데
Speaking of the mass media, I'll write an article about it.
35
give it a try[기브 잇 어 트라이]
한번 해 보다
Give it a try! You can do it.
36
in advance[인 어드밴스]
미리, 사전에
OK. I'll try to study in advance.
